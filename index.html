FAQ

Is this the official Memos app?

No. Memos Life is a third-party client, not affiliated with the Memos project.

How do I connect?

Enter your Memos instance address and sign in with your account or a personal access token. Your password is not stored.

Where does my data go?

Only between the app and your own instance. There is no other server.
