Privacy Policy for Memos Life

Developer: Zangle Lab
Effective date: November 15, 2026
Contact: support@zangle.fun

1. What Memos Life is

Memos Life is a third-party client app for self-hosted Memos instances. It is not an official app of the Memos project. The app does not provide its own cloud service: all of your notes ("memos"), attachments, and account data live on the Memos instance you choose to connect to — typically a server run by yourself, your organization, or someone who invited you.

We (the developer of this app) do not operate that server, and your memo content is never sent to us.

2. Information handled by the app

2.1 Information stored only on your device

  • Instance address (the URL of the Memos server you enter).
  • Access credential (a personal access token) used to sign in to your instance. It is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is removed when you sign out.
  • Your password is never stored. If you sign in with a username and password, the password is used once, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, to obtain an access token from your instance, and is then discarded.
  • Local cache: recent memos and images are cached on your device so the app opens faster and works offline. The cache stays on your device, is cleared when you sign out, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

2.2 Information exchanged with your Memos instance

When you use the app, your device communicates directly with the instance address you provided: your memos, attachments, reactions, comments, and profile information are sent to and stored on that instance only. How that instance stores and processes data is controlled by its operator (which may be you). We have no access to it.

2.3 Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, device information, app interactions with ads, and approximate location. This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

The Pro version contains no ads, and AdMob does not serve or collect advertising data in it.

2.4 Purchases

The Pro upgrade is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google Play provides us only with confirmation of your purchase status (e.g., whether the Pro upgrade is owned), which is used to unlock Pro features and remove ads.

2.5 What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your memos, attachments, or instance credentials.
  • We operate no analytics or user-tracking service of our own, and the app contains no third-party analytics SDK other than Google AdMob (free version) and Google Play services.
  • We do not sell any personal data.

3. Permissions

The app uses network access to talk to your instance and, in the free version, to load ads. Images you attach are selected through the system photo picker, and images you save go to your device's media library — the app does not request broad access to your files.

4. Data retention and deletion

  • Content on your Memos instance: managed by you or your instance administrator; delete it from within the app or the instance at any time.
  • Data on your device: sign out to remove your credential and cache, or uninstall the app to remove everything the app stored locally.

5. Children

Memos Life is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Material changes affecting how data is handled will be reflected in the app's Google Play data safety section as well.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@zangle.fun

Memos Life 隐私政策

开发者：Zangle Lab
生效日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日
联系邮箱：support@zangle.fun

1. Memos Life 是什么

Memos Life 是用于连接自托管 Memos 实例的第三方客户端，不是 Memos 官方应用。本应用不提供自营云服务：你的全部笔记（memos）、附件与账号数据，都保存在你选择连接的那个 Memos 实例上——通常是你自己、你所在组织、或邀请你的人运行的服务器。

我们（本应用开发者）不运营那台服务器，你的笔记内容永远不会发送给我们。

2. 本应用处理的信息

2.1 只保存在你设备上的信息

  • 实例地址（你填写的 Memos 服务器网址）；
  • 访问凭证（个人访问令牌）：仅保存在本机应用的私有存储中，退出登录即删除；
  • 密码永不保存：若用用户名+密码登录，密码只经加密连接（HTTPS）向你的实例使用一次、换取访问令牌后立即丢弃；
  • 本地缓存：最近的笔记与图片会缓存在设备上，以便打开更快、离线可读。缓存只留在你的设备上，退出登录即清除，卸载应用即全部删除。

2.2 与你的 Memos 实例交换的信息

使用本应用时，你的设备直接与你填写的实例地址通信：笔记、附件、表情反应、评论与个人资料只发送并存储在那个实例上。该实例如何存储与处理数据，由其运营者（可能就是你本人）决定，我们无权访问。

2.3 广告（仅免费版）

免费版通过 Google AdMob 展示广告。为投放与衡量广告，Google 可能收集并处理广告 ID、设备信息、你与广告的互动、大致位置等数据，其处理适用 Google 隐私政策。

Pro 版不含广告，AdMob 在其中不投放广告、不收集广告数据。

2.4 购买

Pro 买断由 Google Play 结算处理。我们不接收、不保存你的银行卡信息；Google Play 只向我们提供购买状态确认（如 Pro 是否已拥有），用于解锁 Pro 功能与去除广告。

2.5 我们不收集什么

  • 不收集你的笔记、附件与实例凭证；
  • 我们不运营自有的分析或用户追踪服务；除 Google AdMob（免费版）与 Google Play 服务外，应用不含第三方统计类 SDK；
  • 不出售任何个人数据。

3. 权限

本应用使用网络权限与你的实例通信（免费版另用于加载广告）。添加图片通过系统照片选择器完成，保存图片写入系统相册——本应用不申请对你文件的广泛访问权限。

4. 数据保留与删除

  • 实例上的内容：由你或实例管理员管理，可随时在应用内或实例上删除；
  • 设备上的数据：退出登录即删除凭证与缓存，卸载应用即删除本应用保存的全部本地数据。

5. 儿童

Memos Life 是面向一般用户的效率工具，不针对 13 岁以下儿童。

6. 政策变更

本政策如有变更，我们会更新本页面并修改上方生效日期；涉及数据处理方式的重大变更，会同步反映到 Google Play 的「数据安全」栏目中。

7. 联系方式

对本政策有疑问，请联系：support@zangle.fun
